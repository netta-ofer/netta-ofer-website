looking at looking

2024–2025 · exhibited at GUI/GOOEY and Matter Over Mind

looking at looking at physarum polycephalum

Timelapse video of Physarum polycephalum (slime mold) growth, with the slime mold’s electrical resistance levels mapped to sound recordings of foraging.

looking at looking was exhibited as part of the GUI/GOOEY exhibition in New York and online, curated by Laura Splan; and as part of the Matter Over Mind exhibition at the Dairy Arts Center in Boulder, curated by Stella Witcher.
